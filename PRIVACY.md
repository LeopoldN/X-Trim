# X Trim privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026 — version 0.7.2.

X Trim has no developer-operated backend, analytics, advertising, or error-reporting service. It never sends information to the extension author. Layout, navigation, themes, preferences, and local media-metadata inspection are processed in your browser.

## Optional requests to X

Media downloads request only the image or video you selected from X's media servers (`pbs.twimg.com` and `video.twimg.com`). Firefox asks for optional website-content transmission consent before the extension starts a download. GIF conversion additionally requires optional access to `video.twimg.com`; its MP4 fetch omits credentials. GIF frames are processed locally using the bundled encoder in a temporary extension tab. Conversion output remains local and is saved using Firefox's download API. No media is uploaded to a conversion service. Download requests can reveal the selected media URL and ordinary network information, such as your IP address, to X. Firefox's native download behavior may attach applicable site cookies to direct image/MP4 requests.

Account-detail features and timeline country filtering are off by default. Before enabling them, Firefox asks for optional website-content, browsing-activity, and authentication-information consent. When enabled, the extension sends the displayed account handle and your existing X session credentials only to X (`x.com` or `twitter.com`) to request that account's About information. These requests may happen as you visit supported profiles/posts while the feature is enabled. When timeline country filtering is enabled with a nonempty country list, authors near the viewport are also looked up to compare their reported account location against your saved filters. Filters and exempt handles stay in local storage. Unknown locations are left visible. The extension does not ask you to enter passwords. Authentication/CSRF headers are held only in memory, are not logged or saved, and are cleared when the tab closes or account details are disabled or consent is revoked.

You may decline or revoke optional consent in Firefox's extension permissions. Chrome has no built-in data-collection consent, so in the Chrome version X Trim asks through its own popup buttons and switches, saves your answer in its local storage, and stops the related requests when you turn those features off. The affected remote features stop; local layout and theme features remain usable. Disable both account details and timeline country filtering in X Trim’s popup to stop automatic account-detail requests. Optional host access and data consent are distinct permissions.

## Local storage and retention

Preferences remain in Firefox extension storage. Account-detail responses from normal windows are cached locally for up to 24 hours, with at most 100 records; expired records are ignored and pruned on subsequent writes. Private-window account responses are not written to disk. While account details are enabled and consent is granted, profile counts in X’s existing GraphQL responses are read locally and held in memory for up to 100 accounts per tab; this cache is cleared when the tab closes, account details are disabled, or consent is revoked. Media URLs are cached in memory, at most 100 per tab, and cleared when downloads are disabled or the tab closes. Temporary GIF input/output data is released after conversion/download or cancellation. Files you choose to save and Firefox's own download history remain under your control.

Removing the extension removes its extension storage. It does not delete files you saved. X's own data handling is governed by X's policies.

While hiding promoted or Paid partnership posts, or filtering Premium replies, is enabled, X Trim reads which posts are promoted or labelled as paid partnerships, and whether their authors are Premium, business or government verified or followed by you, from X’s existing responses. These facts stay in page memory (up to 5,000 posts) and are never sent anywhere. Keyword filters stay in local storage. The optional daily time limit keeps one number in local storage: today’s minutes on X, which resets each day. Keep Following sorted by Recent changes one setting (`enableRanking`) on X’s own Following timeline request; no other request is added or changed. Fast block, off by default, accepts X’s own Block confirmation for you after you choose Block.

When the Block shortcut is enabled, block status is read locally from X’s existing responses and retained only in page memory (up to 1,000 handles). It is used to color already-blocked accounts red. No extra lookup requests are made or block list saved.
